Questions?

Common Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

What is a Farm Share?

A Farm Share gives members a rotating selection of local, seasonal food while supporting participating farmers and producers.

Can I use SNAP / EBT?

Yes. The Local Co-op accepts SNAP / EBT. Contact the Co-op if you need help with payment options.

Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup options currently include McNeal, Bisbee and Douglas. Available locations may depend on your order or Farm Share.

What if I need help with my account or order?

Call The Local Co-op at (520) 261-2255 and we'll help you.
