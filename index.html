Know Your Food

Our Farmers & Producers

Meet some of the people growing, raising and producing food for our Southern Arizona community.

John

Desert Sky Produce

A McNeal farm helping strengthen local food access and opportunities for small producers throughout Cochise County.

MEET THE FARMER

John Benedict moved to McNeal in 2018 and founded Desert Sky Produce in late 2020. Seeing the need for stronger local food access and better outlets for small farmers, ranchers and producers, he went on to co-found The Local Co-op.

His community work has included produce-prescription programs, the McNeal food bank, the Cochise Mobile Market and Cochise Farm to School, connecting local agriculture with food access, education and community health.

Jillian

Likha's Veggies

A growing local farm built from hands-on learning, community roots and a love of fresh produce.

MEET THE FARMER

Jillian's farming journey began in 2021 while volunteering at the Seven Arrows Recovery community garden. She later leased her own plot and began sharing what she grew at the Bisbee Farmers Market.

After apprenticing with local farms and learning both the art and science of sustainable agriculture, she began building Likha's Veggies into a thriving 2-acre farm focused on feeding the community.

Saudi

Lutu Muhammad Farm

A first-generation family produce farm growing salad greens, herbs and grains with an emphasis on clean cultivation.

MEET THE FARMER

Saudi and his family operate Lutu Muhammad Farm, a first-generation family-owned produce farm focused on lettuce and salad greens, herbs and grains.

According to the farm's Local Co-op profile, they do not use synthetic pesticides and do not plant non-GMO seeds, reflecting their focus on producing food they consider pure, clean and nourishing.

Weedy & Family

Weidenthal Family Farm

A family farm near the San Pedro River raising poultry and heritage pigs while developing a regenerative permaculture orchard.

MEET THE FARMER

Weidenthal Family Farm began as a small backyard operation in spring 2020 with Pekin ducks and Jumbo quail. It later expanded to 20 acres near the San Pedro River in Sierra Vista.

The family focuses on regenerative agriculture and is developing a 2-acre permaculture orchard. Ducks and chickens graze among the orchard rows, while heritage pigs help clear low shrubs in areas planned for future expansion.

Questions?

Common Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

What is a Farm Share?

A Farm Share gives members a rotating selection of local seasonal food while supporting participating farmers and producers.

Can I use SNAP / EBT?

Yes. The Local Co-op accepts SNAP / EBT for eligible purchases. Contact the Co-op if you need help with payment options.

Can I pay with EBT in person?

An EBT pay-in-person option may be selected when available. Bring your EBT card when you pick up your order.

Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup options currently include McNeal, Bisbee and Douglas. Available pickup locations can depend on your order or Farm Share.

What if I need help with my account or order?

Call The Local Co-op at (520) 261-2255 and we'll help you.
