Make an Impact

Support The Local Co-op's mission to expand access to fresh, local food for community members who need it most.

One-time contributions and monthly support help strengthen food access efforts in our community.

Where Your Contribution Goes

Helping Our Neighbors

🥕

Emergency Food Relief

Urgent food assistance for families facing difficult circumstances, including homebound neighbors without transportation.

🧺

Free Food Distribution

Support weekly free food distributions, including pantry staples and fresh food for community members in need.

🤝

Expand Food Access

Help build partnerships, referral systems and outreach that connect more neighbors with nutritious food.

Ways to Help

Every Contribution Matters

The Co-op's existing contribution page describes these example amounts and their intended impact.

$10Fresh produce and staples
$25More than half a food box
$50A food box plus packing and transport help
$100Two food boxes

Custom amounts and monthly support are also described in the existing contribution program.

Important: The Local Co-op states that it is not a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Contributions are not tax deductible.
